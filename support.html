Support

Babel writes books for you on your iPhone or iPad. Here's how to get the most from it — and how to reach us.

Getting started

1

Ask for a book

Type an idea in "What would you like to read?" and tap Your idea, or tap any cover under Discover.

2

Watch it being written

Babel plans six chapters, then writes Chapter 1 onto the page. Start reading as soon as it appears.

3

Keep going

At the end of a chapter, tap Write Chapter 2. Your place is saved automatically.

Frequently asked questions

Does Babel need an internet connection or an account?

No. The AI that writes your books is built into the app and runs entirely on your device. Babel works in airplane mode and never asks you to sign in.

Why does writing take a while?

Every word is written by an AI model running on your device's own processor, not on a server. Planning takes a few seconds; a chapter can take a minute or two. Newer iPhones and iPads are noticeably faster.

You don't have to wait for a chapter to finish — start reading as soon as the first paragraph appears.

What happens if I leave a book while it's being written?

Writing stops the moment you go back or open another book, so it never runs in the background. Everything written so far is kept.

When you return, scroll to the end and tap Finish Chapter to complete it.

Search shows only "Your idea". Where are the suggestions?

Suggestions take a few seconds to appear — you'll see placeholder covers and "Babel is finding books for you…" while they load. Your own idea is ready straight away.

If you open a book before suggestions arrive, the search stops so the book can start sooner. Search again to see them.

How do I stop or clear a search?

While Babel is looking for books, the search field shows an orange Stop button — tap it to stop straight away. Then tap ✕ to clear the search and return to the home page, or press Return to search again.

You can also change the text at any time and press Return; the new search replaces the old one.

How do I change the text size or switch to the dark theme?

In a book, tap Aa at the top right for Contents, text size, theme, Copy and Share. On the home screen, the moon/sun button switches themes.

How do I jump to another chapter?

Tap Aa › Contents and pick a chapter. Chapters that haven't been written yet are greyed out.

The top bar disappeared while I was reading.

A tap on the page hides the bars so you can read without distractions. Tap the page again to bring them back.

How do I remove a book from my library?

On the home screen, press and hold the book's cover under Your Library and choose Remove from Library.

Where are my books stored? Will I lose them?

Your books are stored only on your device and saved as they're written, so they survive the app being closed. They are deleted if you delete Babel.

Like other app data, they can be included in your own iCloud or computer backup of the device, and restored with it.

Is what Babel writes accurate?

Babel's books are generated by AI. Treat them as reading for enjoyment and ideas, not as a reference: they can contain mistakes, invented facts, and inconsistencies between chapters.

Nothing Babel writes is medical, legal, financial or other professional advice.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad running iOS / iPadOS 16.4 or later. Babel needs about 800 MB of free storage for the built-in AI model.
